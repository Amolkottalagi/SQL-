What is this?

SQL LLM lets you ask questions about a database in plain English. A language model writes a SQL query, the app checks it for safety, runs it, and shows you the query and the result, as a table or a chart.

It was built as a project on natural-language-to-SQL, comparing prompting strategies (zero-shot and few-shot) with an Evaluation page for researchers.

How a question is answered

  1. Your question is checked against your daily plan limits.
  2. The database structure (and a few sample rows) is sent to the AI with your question.
  3. The AI replies with one SQL query and a short explanation, or asks you for clarification.
  4. The query is checked: only a single read-only SELECT is allowed. It then runs on a read-only database account with a time limit and row cap.
  5. If the query fails, the AI gets one chance to fix it automatically.

Tips for good questions

  • Name the thing you want counted or listed: "Total revenue per product".
  • Add a time range or filter if you need one: "Orders in July".
  • If an answer looks wrong, press 👎 and say why. It helps improve the prompts.
  • Past queries can be re-run for free from History or Saved queries.

Privacy

  • Your questions and the SQL generated for them are stored so you can see your history, and for research on query quality.
  • The question, the database structure and a few sample rows are sent to the AI provider. Query results are not.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • When you delete your account, your login and settings are removed. Your past questions stay in the system without your name.
